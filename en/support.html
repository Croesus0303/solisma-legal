Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get tarot readings?

Open the Tarotum app, select a spread type (Three Card, Celtic Cross, Relationship, etc.), write your question, and draw your cards. Each reading uses one credit.

What are credits and how do they work?

Credits are used to request tarot readings. Each spread costs 1 credit regardless of the number of cards. You can earn free credits when you start using the app, purchase credit packs, or subscribe for monthly allowances.

How do I check my remaining credits?

Your credit balance is displayed in the app on the home screen and profile page. Subscription users can see their remaining monthly allowance in the same locations.

What's the difference between credits and subscriptions?
  • Credit Packs: One-time purchases that never expire. Use them whenever you want.
  • Subscriptions: Monthly plans that give you a set number of readings per month. Unused readings don't roll over.
How do I access Astro-Tarot readings?

Astro-Tarot readings (12 Houses) require an active subscription. You'll need to provide your birth date, time, and place to calculate your astrological birth chart. Subscription tiers include different numbers of astro readings per month.

Are my readings private?

Yes, your readings are stored securely in your personal account and are only accessible to you. We do not share individual readings with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Manage Your Subscription

To view, change, or cancel your Tarotum subscription:

On iPhone/iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Tap your Apple ID (your name at the top)
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Tarotum
  5. From here you can:
    • Change your subscription tier
    • Cancel your subscription
    • View billing history

Note: After canceling, you'll continue to have access to subscription features until the end of your current billing period.

Request a Refund

All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. To request a refund:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID
  3. Find the Tarotum purchase
  4. Select "Request a refund" and explain the issue

Apple will review your request and determine eligibility. We cannot process refunds directly.

Delete Your Account

If you wish to delete your Tarotum account and all associated data:

Option 1: In the App

  1. Open Tarotum
  2. Go to Profile or Settings
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm your choice

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to support@tarotum.app with:

  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • The email address associated with your account

We will process your request within 30 days.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent. All your reading history, credits, and data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Active subscriptions must be canceled separately through Apple.

Troubleshooting

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Make sure you have the latest version of Tarotum installed
  • Try closing and reopening the app
  • Restart your device
  • Check that you have a stable internet connection

Purchases Not Appearing

  • Wait a few minutes and check again - purchases may take time to process
  • Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase
  • Try the "Restore Purchases" option in the app settings
  • Check your email for a receipt from Apple to confirm the purchase went through

Reading Not Loading

  • Check your internet connection
  • Wait a few moments - readings may take a moment to generate
  • If you see an error, your credit will be automatically refunded
  • Try closing and reopening the app

Notifications Not Working

  • Open iPhone Settings > Notifications > Tarotum
  • Make sure "Allow Notifications" is enabled
  • Check that you haven't muted the app
